Solo vs. Group Adventures: Which One Is Right for You?

By Elena Rostova • 5 min read

We built this company around the idea that traveling solo shouldn't mean traveling alone. That distinction is basically the whole answer to this question, but it's worth breaking down properly, because solo and group trips genuinely give you different things, and neither one is the better option across the board.

What Solo Travel Actually Gives You

Nobody to negotiate the schedule with. You linger at the waterfall as long as you want, skip the market stop if you're tired, decide on a whim to add an extra day in Kampot. Solo travel also has a way of pushing you into conversations you wouldn't otherwise have, with guides, with other travelers, with people in the village you're passing through.
